# A journey through the valley

Early one morning, Maya left the village with a small bag and a map. The road followed a river that flowed between the hills.

Walking beside the water, she noticed that the birds were unusually quiet. She stopped to listen before crossing the old wooden bridge.

## Why did Maya change her plan?

The bridge had been damaged by heavy rain. Although she wanted to reach the town before sunset, she decided to take the longer path.

## What helped her find another route?

A farmer showed her a narrow track through the fields. By asking a simple question, Maya found a safer way to continue her journey.
